# Audio Adaptation: Sound Generation via ALD-SC

> This document specifies the changes to the ALD-SC architecture needed
> to extend it from image generation to raw audio synthesis. The research
> programme (see [`00.md`](00.md) § "The research programme") is
> modality-agnostic and carries over unchanged. This document covers the
> audio-specific adaptations: the audio representation, the 1-D
> architecture, the controlled experiment, and the evaluation.

## 1. Motivation

Sound (speech, environmental audio, short tonal clips) has simpler
spectral statistics than music — fewer simultaneous harmonic layers, no
long-range rhythmic structure to model. This makes it the right testbed
to validate the core claim: **decoding on the feature-space manifold
beats decoding on an unconstrained latent for audio**, before adding
musical complexity.

## 2. Audio representation: EnCodec latents

A pre-trained audio VAE/codec (EnCodec, 24 kHz mono) is used as a
**frozen feature extractor**. Its pre-quantization continuous encoder
features serve as the 1-D diffusion latent $z$.

### Why pre-quantization continuous features?

Diffusion requires a continuous latent space. EnCodec's quantized codes
are discrete (RVQ indices), which are unsuitable for v-prediction
diffusion. The pre-quantization encoder output — a continuous
$(B, D, T)$ feature tensor — is the natural latent for diffusion.

### Shapes

| Symbol | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|
| $x$ | $(B, 1, L)$ | Raw audio waveform (24 kHz mono) |
| $z$ | $(B, 128, T)$ | EnCodec continuous latent (D=128) |
| $T$ | $L / 320$ | Frame rate 75 Hz (stride 320) |
| $A$ | $(B, 128)$ | Pooled features $= \text{mean}_T(z)$ |
| $c_{\text{spec}}$ | $(B, 3q)$ | Spectral chart conditioning vector |

For a 5-second clip at 24 kHz: $L = 120\,000$, $T = 375$.

### Feature embeddings for the prior

`build_arrow_prior()` requires $(N, F)$ corpus embeddings, not raw
audio. We extract these by pooling EnCodec encoder features over time for
each clip in the corpus:

$$
\text{embeddings} = \{ \text{mean}_T(z_i) \}_{i=1}^{N} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 128}
$$

This gives $F = 128$ (matching EnCodec's encoder dimension). The
ArrowSpace prior is then built once from this matrix via kNN over feature
columns.

## 3. Architecture changes

### 3.1 1-D DiT (`dit.py`)

The 2-D patchify (`Conv2d` over spatial grid → square tokens) is replaced
with 1-D patchify:

- `Conv1d(latent_channels, dim, kernel_size=patch, stride=patch)` over $T$
- Positional embedding: `pos_embed (1, num_patches, dim)` where
  `num_patches = T // patch_size`
- Unpatchify: `Linear(dim, latent_channels * patch_size)` → reshape to
  $(B, C, T)$

The AdaLN conditioning scaffold, CFG dropout, and time embedding are
unchanged. For Phase 1, the DiT is **unconditional** (time-only AdaLN);
the `c_spec` and `text_emb` hooks are retained but default off.

### 3.2 1-D graph decoder (`graph_decoder.py`)

The `WaveReconstructionBlock`, `GraphDecoder`, and
`ClockGatedGraphDecoder` are rewritten for 1-D:

- `Conv2d` → `Conv1d`
- `mean(dim=(2, 3))` (spatial pooling) → `mean(dim=2)` (temporal pooling)
- 2-D `interpolate` → 1-D `interpolate`

The **project→gate→lift core** is dim-agnostic and transfers unchanged:
1. $\hat{H} = A \cdot U_q$ (project to chart)
2. $g = \sigma(W \cdot c_{\text{spec}})$ (gate by dispersion)
3. $A' = (\hat{H} \odot g) \cdot U_q^\top$ (lift back)

### 3.3 Upsampling: matching EnCodec's stride

EnCodec 24 kHz uses encoder strides $(2, 4, 5, 8)$ for a total downsampling
factor of $320\times$. The decoder mirrors this with 4 upsampling stages
of the same factors, producing $320\times$ temporal upsampling:

$$
T_{\text{latent}} \xrightarrow{\times 2} \xrightarrow{\times 4}
\xrightarrow{\times 5} \xrightarrow{\times 8} T_{\text{audio}}
$$

For $T = 375$: $375 \times 320 = 120\,000$ samples = 5 s @ 24 kHz.

### 3.4 Audio codec module (`audio_codec.py`)

New module composing the frozen encoder and trainable decoders:

- `EnCodecEncoder`: frozen wrapper, `encode(x) → (z, A, c_spec)` where
  $A = \text{mean}_T(z)$ and $c_{\text{spec}} = \text{prior.chart\_energy\_descriptor}(A)$
- `BaselineAudioDecoder`: matched-capacity 1-D conv decoder (same
  channels and upsampling as `GraphDecoder`, but plain `ResBlock1d`
  instead of `WaveReconstructionBlock` — no $U_q$, no $\lambda^{\text{ED}}$)
- `AudioVAE`: composes encoder + graph or baseline decoder

### 3.5 Data loading (`data.py`)

- `Esc50Dataset`: loads ESC-50 clips, resamples to 24 kHz mono, normalizes
- `AudioFolderDataset`: generic audio folder loader
- `ToyAudioDataset`: synthetic waveforms (sine/noise) for testing

## 4. c_spec derivation: a simplification

In the image pipeline, `c_spec` comes from a separate feature head on the
encoder, and is randomly sampled at generation time. For audio, since
EnCodec is a frozen feature extractor (not a trainable dual-head
encoder), `c_spec` is **derived from $z$ itself**:

$$
A = \text{mean}_T(z), \quad c_{\text{spec}} = \text{prior.chart\_energy\_descriptor}(A)
$$

This makes the decoder **self-consistent** at both training and
generation time — no separate `c_spec` sampling needed. The DiT
generates $z$ unconditionally, then the decoder derives $c_{\text{spec}}$
from the generated $z$.

## 5. Conditioning (deferred to Phase 2)

No text/genre/tag conditioning in Phase 1 — unconditional generation only,
to isolate whether the graph-structured decoder itself improves
reconstruction fidelity over an unconstrained baseline. Text/CLAP
conditioning is explicitly out of scope and tracked in a follow-up issue
for music-specific generation.

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Reconstruction fidelity

Compare graph decoder vs baseline decoder on held-out ESC-50 clips:

- **Waveform L1**: $\|x - \hat{x}\|_1$
- **Multi-scale STFT loss**: spectral convergence + log-magnitude L1
  across multiple FFT sizes (512, 1024, 2048)
- **Spectral diagnostics**: chart-energy error, off-manifold ratio
  (from the prior, dim-agnostic)
- **EnCodec-native reference**: EnCodec's own encoder→decoder
  reconstruction reported as a ceiling

### 6.2 Fréchet Audio Distance (FAD)

FAD between generated samples and real ESC-50 clips, computed via VGGish
embeddings (using `fadtk`). Compare:
- $\text{FAD}_{\text{graph}}$ (graph decoder samples vs real)
- $\text{FAD}_{\text{baseline}}$ (baseline decoder samples vs real)

### 6.3 Ablation: with vs without $\lambda^{\text{ED}}$ gating

Graph decoder with full $c_{\text{spec}}$ gating vs with gates disabled
($c_{\text{spec}} = 0$). This mirrors the image pipeline's `06_sampling`
notebook with-vs-without $c_{\text{spec}}$ ablation.

## 7. What transfers unchanged from the image pipeline

These modules are dimension-agnostic and require no changes:

| Module | Why it transfers |
|---|---|
| `arrow_prior.py` | Operates on $(N, F)$ and $(B, F)$ — feature-axis only |
| `build_prior.py` | Corpus-level $(N, F)$ → $L_F$, $U_q$, $\lambda^{\text{ED}}$ |
| `wire_graph.py` | ArrowSpace adapter over $(N, F)$ |
| `dual_space.py` | 2.5-D matrix $M_N$ from $(N, F)$ embeddings |
| `schedule.py` | `add_noise`/`v_target` use rank-agnostic broadcasting |
| `spectral_schedule.py` | Per-mode $\tau_k(t)$, $\bar\alpha_k(t)$ — spectrum only |
| `losses.py` | `L_chart`, `L_smooth` delegate to prior (feature-axis); `L_rec` extended with STFT |

## 8. What is replaced

| Module | Image version | Audio version |
|---|---|---|
| `dit.py` | `Conv2d` patchify, square latent | `Conv1d` patchify, 1-D latent |
| `graph_decoder.py` | `Conv2d`, 2-D pooling/interpolate | `Conv1d`, 1-D pooling/interpolate |
| `sampling.py` | `randn(B, C, s, s)` (square) | `randn(B, C, T)` (1-D) |
| `vae.py` | Trainable dual-head encoder | Removed — EnCodec replaces encoder |
| `data.py` | `ImageFolderDataset`, `ToyImageDataset` | `Esc50Dataset`, `AudioFolderDataset`, `ToyAudioDataset` |
| `trainer.py` | `train_vae()`, `train_diffusion()` | `train_audio_decoder()`, `train_audio_diffusion()` |

## 9. Dataset: ESC-50

ESC-50 is a labeled collection of 2000 environmental audio clips (50
classes, 40 clips per class, 5 seconds each). It is small, homogeneous
within classes, and publicly available — ideal for building a tractable
ArrowSpace graph.

- 2000 clips, 5 s each, 44.1 kHz (resampled to 24 kHz mono)
- 50 classes: animals, natural soundscapes, human non-speech, interior/
  domestic, exterior/urban
- Download: https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50

## 10. Risks

- **Decoder capacity**: a from-scratch 320× waveform decoder on 2000 clips
  will likely lag EnCodec's native decoder in absolute quality. The
  **graph-vs-baseline controlled comparison** (same capacity, only graph
  structure differs) remains valid regardless.
- **EnCodec weights**: auto-downloaded on first use (~hundreds of MB).
  Tests skip gracefully without network access.
- **FAD dependency**: `fadtk` provides VGGish-based FAD without
  TensorFlow. Fallback: multi-scale STFT distance if FAD deps are
  undesirable.
